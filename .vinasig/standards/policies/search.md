# SEO, AEO and GEO

SEARCH-001 MUST review public indexable pages for status, meaningful title/description, canonical, indexing policy, sitemap, internal links, rendered HTML, social metadata and relevant structured data. Validate JSON-LD syntax and its truthfulness against visible content; use Google's Rich Results Test for Google features and Schema.org Validator for vocabulary. Neither guarantees rich results. Check reciprocal hreflang only when multiple language URLs exist. Do not fabricate entities, authors, reviews or experiences.

SEARCH-002 SHOULD answer actual audience questions with useful, sourced and original content, readable headings and consistent identity. AEO/GEO work builds on technical SEO and content quality. Do not create templated content or fake mentions to influence answers. Do not make llms.txt, a special AI schema, an API or WebMCP a universal discoverability requirement. Google's current guide does not require these files for its generative search features. They may serve a documented non-Google consumer.

Only the owner-approved indexing/crawler policy controls robots, noindex and bot access. Search crawlers and training crawlers have different purposes. Bulk URL/canonical changes, indexing submissions and new analytics need task authorization. Existing public URLs need a migration/redirect and internal-link plan when changed.

Use local metadata/link tests and Lighthouse SEO first. Add a bounded crawl for larger sites. Screaming Frog's official MCP is a paid feature. Search Console and Bing require property/account access. AlsoAsked informs questions; Ahrefs Brand Radar/Semrush measure vendor-specific visibility. They are optional, not substitutes for correctness.

Measure technical errors, content gaps, brand mentions, URL citations, referrals and conversions separately. Record model/platform, questions, locale, market, sample/date and uncertainty. Private dashboards or unrun scans are NOT_RUN. Never guarantee rank, traffic or citations.
